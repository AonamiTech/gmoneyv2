This adjudication is independent-first. First inspect every supplied page image and make
a complete fresh visual annotation in your working process without opening or relying on
`INDEPENDENT_A_JSON`, `INDEPENDENT_B_JSON`, OCR, parser output, model output, a baseline,
or any other machine output. Do not request, inspect, or use machine outputs at any
stage. Do not guess blurred, occluded, clipped, or ambiguous text: use `readable: false`,
leave the value null, and give a short visual reason in `note`. Preserve printed spelling
and punctuation. Never calculate, reconcile, or invent a rate, quantity, amount, subtotal,
tax, balance, or total; record a total only when visibly printed.

Only after completing that independent first reading, compare it with the two supplied
Luna review JSON objects. Resolve disagreements by returning to the page pixels, not by
majority vote or arithmetic. Record every material disagreement in the final visual
annotation, including an unreadable decision when the pixels do not support a value.
`STRUCTURAL_ID_MAP_JSON` is produced from the pre-gold SourceManifest geometry pass; it
is not machine transcription. Structural IDs are generated by the validator; do not
invent or hand-compute IDs. Use only IDs from the map for continuation links and total
scopes. Return visible `raw_value` only and leave `normalized_value` null because
normalization belongs to the evaluator. Leave row numeric projections (`rate`,
`quantity`, `gross_amount`, `discount`, and `amount`) and total `normalized_value` null;
preserve a visible printed number only in its raw field.
